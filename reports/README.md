## Stakeholder review notes

The report template was reviewed with engineering stakeholders and approved with a few clarifications:

- Keep the report to one page of key outcomes, active work, blockers, and next steps.
- Use a single overall status label for the team or project: Green, Amber, or Red.
- Distinguish milestones and work in progress from operational risks and dependencies.
- Include direct links or issue references where useful so readers can drill into detail quickly.

## Confirmed metrics and status definitions

- Overall status is based on delivery health for the reporting period, not just the number of tasks completed.
- Green: work is on track, dependencies are either resolved or low risk.
- Amber: key work is progressing, but there are known risks or timing concerns.
- Red: delivery is materially at risk or blocked, and action is needed from leadership or stakeholders.
- Metrics should reflect the current reporting period and highlight only the most relevant outcomes and risks.
