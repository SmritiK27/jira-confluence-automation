# How to Fill Out the Weekly Status Report

1. Copy `template.md` for the reporting period and keep the headings unchanged.
2. Enter the reporting dates, team or project name, report owner, and overall status.
3. Under **Accomplishments**, list meaningful work completed during the period and describe outcomes when useful.
4. Under **In Progress**, list active work with a clear owner and target date.
5. Under **Blockers and Risks**, describe anything that could delay delivery, its impact, and the next action.
6. Under **Next Period's Plan**, list the highest-priority work planned for the next period and assign owners.
7. Under **Decisions or Support Needed**, record requests for leadership or stakeholder action and include the needed-by date.
8. Use concise bullet points, link to relevant tickets or documents, and remove unused placeholders before sharing.
